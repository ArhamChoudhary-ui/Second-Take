# Demo script — target 2:40

This is a recording plan, not a measured video duration. Rehearse on the actual device and connection. Show the real app with the guided-mode and simulation labels visible. No live Alexa or Bedrock access is claimed.

## Before recording

Open `/demo`, start **The missing confirmation**, and use one browser tab. Other tabs with the same cookies share the practice workspace. Close unrelated tabs. Choose a zoom level that makes the plan and evidence readable. Use your own narration without background music.

## 0:00–0:15 — The problem

Say: “The camera is reserved. The assistant thinks it failed. Should it book again? Second Take helps an assistant check what happened, finish safely, and respect your changes.”

Show the selected missing-confirmation story and click **Preview the three actions**.

## 0:15–0:40 — Approval and failure

Say: “One request creates a plan for two workshop places, a camera, and a calendar event. Nothing is booked until I approve. This example deliberately drops the camera confirmation.”

Review the plan, click **Approve & book**, and show the paused result. Keep the controlled-failure label visible.

## 0:40–1:05 — Evidence

Say: “Look at the two views. The service has saved the camera. The assistant has no confirmation, and the calendar has not run. A timeout does not tell us whether the action happened.”

Show **What actually happened**. Point to the camera’s saved record and missing confirmation, then the pending calendar row.

## 1:05–1:25 — Recovery

Click **Check & resume**.

Say: “Second Take checks the original reservation identity. It finds the existing camera and completes the calendar. One camera record, zero duplicates. These numbers come from this run’s saved records.”

Show the completed story and evidence table. Refresh once if timing allows; the saved story persists.

## 1:25–2:05 — The human edit

Start **The edit that stays yours**. Preview and approve the booking. Click **Write my calendar edit**, save the visible meeting-point note, then **Preview removing the camera**.

Say: “I add a meeting point myself, then change my mind about the camera. The assistant detects my newer calendar version. I keep my edit and approve only the remaining cancellation.”

Click **Keep my calendar edit**, then **Approve these changes**. Show the preserved text and active workshop. Shorten narration if requests are slow; never replace a failed result with a staged success.

## 2:05–2:25 — Useful output

Download and open the readable receipt. Point out saved records, action attempts and preserved text. Optionally show **Calendar file**.

Say: “The receipt explains what changed and what stayed. The calendar file is a demo-event snapshot I can import. It is not live calendar sync.”

## 2:25–2:40 — Technical close

Say: “This software-only Alexa+ simulation calls a real MCP Streamable HTTP endpoint. Our backend enforces plan approval, stable identities and version checks. The booking service is a sample; the recovery logic and persistence work. Second Take: change your mind, keep control.”

Show the About dialog or MCP route in the repository, without credentials. End on the completed story.

## Optional alternative scene

The third story demonstrates a partial cancellation: the calendar updates, the camera cancellation fails, then only that cancellation retries. Substitute it for the manual-edit section or keep it for a longer follow-up walkthrough.
