# Five-person evaluation protocol

## Goal

Find out whether a person understands and can control the assistant's changes. Do not claim results until sessions occur.

## Participants and consent

Recruit five adults who book activities or appointments online. Explain that this is an isolated demo, there are no purchases, and they should not enter sensitive information. Ask permission before recording. Participation and feedback can be anonymous.

## Tasks

1. Book two workshop places, a camera, and a calendar event.
2. Change your mind about the camera while preserving workshop seats.
3. Recover a lost camera-service response.
4. Edit the calendar and respond to the resulting assistant conflict.
5. Explain which records remain and whether recovery created a duplicate.

Avoid coaching during the attempt. Offer help after a participant says they are stuck; record it.

Ask: “What do you think happened?”, “What is still uncertain?”, and “What would you expect that button to do?” Record exact quotes only with permission.

Report the denominator, task definition, assistance, and failures. Do not extrapolate a five-person study to general population performance. Use findings to fix confusing wording and missing states before adding features.
